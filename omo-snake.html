4.1.1.4. Arts
4.1.1.4.1. Films
Brave AI
Q
  • Top 20 Nagin Or Snakes Hindi Films Are
A
  • Based on the search results, here are the top Hindi snake movies (Nagin/Nagin films), ranked by historical impact and critical mentions:
  • Nagina (1986): Starring Sridevi, Rishi Kapoor, and Amrish Puri, this is widely considered the biggest blockbuster of the genre, defining the visual and narrative standards for Bollywood snake films.
  • Nagin (1976): A multi-starrer fantasy thriller starring Reena Roy, Sunil Dutt, and Feroz Khan, known for its immediate revenge plot where a snake-woman kills hunters who killed her mate.
  • Hisss (2010): Directed by Jennifer Lynch and starring Mallika Sherawat and Irrfan Khan, this modern entry features a seductive snake-woman seeking revenge and the Naagmani.
  • Sheshnaag (1990): A star-studded fantasy featuring Jeetendra, Rekha, Rishi Kapoor, and Danny Denzongpa, focusing on wish-fulfilling snakes pursued by an Aghori.
  • Nigahen (1989): The sequel to Nagina, starring Sridevi and Sunny Deol, though it did not replicate the massive success of its predecessor.
  • Vishkanya (1991): Starring Pooja Bedi and Kunal Goswami, this film depicts a woman transformed into a venomous killer (Vishkanya) to avenge her parents.
  • Jaani Dushman (1979): A classic revenge thriller starring Sanjeev Kumar, Rekha, and Sunil Dutt, featuring a serial killer with a snake fetish.
  • Tum Mere Ho (1990): Uniquely starring Aamir Khan as a snake-charmer with superpowers, co-starring Juhi Chawla in a rural love story.
  • Nache Nagin Gali Gali (1989): Starring Meenakshi Seshadri and Nitish Bharadwaj, this obscure hit features a separated snake couple reunited through vengeance.
  • Doodh Ka Karz (1990): A revenge drama where Neelam Kothari plays a snake-charmer's widow who raises her son and a loyal snake to avenge her husband's death.
  • Naag Nagin (1989): Starring Rajiv Kapoor and Mandakini, this film failed at the box office despite its star power.
  • Nagin (1954): An early classic starring Vyjayanthimala and Pradeep Kumar, focusing on lovers from warring snake-charming tribes.
  • Jaani Dushman: Ek Anokhi Kahani (2002): A later remake featuring Sunny Deol and Manisha Koirala, where a shape-shifting snake seeks vengeance on friends.
  • Naagmani (1991): A fantasy film starring Rakesh Bedi, centered on a sorcerer trying to capture a sacred stone.
  • Neeya (1979): A Tamil film starring Kamal Haasan that is a remake of the 1976 Hindi movie Nagin.
  • Nagin: The Snake Woman (2010): An Indo-American thriller based on the Far Eastern myth of a shape-shifting snake woman.
  • Naag Padmini (1957): An early entry in the genre listed among the classic snake films.
  • Naag Devta (1962): A notable film from the early era of Indian snake cinema.
  • Naag Mohini (1963): Another classic from the 1960s featuring shape-shifting snakes.
  • Naag Champa (1976): A film from the same era as the 1976 Nagin, part of the early wave of snake-themed movies.
  • Other notable mentions include Naag Panchami (1972), Naag Mera Saathi (1973), Naag Devta (1962), and Neeli Aankhen (starring Rubina Fasihuddin as a blue-eyed snake-woman). The genre heavily features the motif of the Ichchadhari (wish-fulfilling) snake seeking the Naagmani gem for power or immortality.
